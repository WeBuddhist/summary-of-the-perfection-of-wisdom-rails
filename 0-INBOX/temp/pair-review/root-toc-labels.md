# Root-text section headings (Derge TOC) rendered into Sanskrit and Chinese

Row numbers refer to `bo-sa-pairs.md` and `bo-zh-pairs.md` in this folder (row N of each file = row N of the Tibetan). "Not attested" = the term is the standard equivalent, but the compound does not occur in that translation; attested component words are listed.

## Notes

- Tibetan བསྟན་པ (teaching) is left out of the Sanskrit and Chinese headings to keep them short. If wanted, use -उपदेशः / -निर्देशः (उपदेश is attested in प्रज्ञोपदेश, row 368; देशयन्ति rows 7, 8, 366) and 說 / 示 (not checked in the file).
- Only sa has attested terms for headings 1 and 3 (and 6 in part); zh attests 一切智 (3) and 漸次 (6) only. Heading 1 in zh has no attested equivalent: the zh chapter title 行品第一 (row 4) names the chapter (caryā), not the topic.
- Chinese chapter titles 2–11 (rows 34–121) carry a different scheme (地 / 波羅蜜, e.g. 歡喜地攝布施波羅蜜); it does not map onto the eight topics.
- Both translations lack the topic markers: the eight headings occur only in the Tibetan (rows 6, 34, 101, 110, 229, 363, 367; heading 8 inside row 367).
