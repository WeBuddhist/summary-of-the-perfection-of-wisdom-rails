## Notes
- No long run of rows is shifted by one. The alignment is consistent from 5 to 368, including the long mid-section (rows 134–330), where I compared images and numbers row by row (Ganges sand, Meru, ship, pot, king, merchant, bird and similar similes).
- Rows 36–39 are the only place where several Tibetan rows (36–39, four verses) map onto fewer Chinese rows (ZH 36–37, with ZH 38 and ZH 39 empty). Fix by moving ZH 37 down to BO 38 (or merging BO 37–39 against ZH 36–37).
- Rows 27–29 (and, more weakly, 153/154) show half-verse slips where a two-line half belongs to the neighbouring row. In 153/154 the last two lines of ZH 153 ("不見具信諸菩薩 遠般若行求退墮") are the Chinese for the start of BO 154. This is mild looseness, not a pairing error.
- The Chinese is much shorter than the Tibetan in several rows (88, 129–131, 154, 322, 52), and the Chinese drops or merges lines. I did not treat this as an error.
- The Chinese repeats text in rows 26/27 and 135/136, and row 360 contains a stray "I" and row 340 a stray "‘". These look like transcription slips in the zh doc.
- Tibetan-side noise (not a pairing issue): stray Latin letters in rows 51, 60, 71, 292 (གāའི), row 359 (བཻḍūརྱ), a "༼༥༽" marker in row 302, and a missing final shad in 210.
- Row 310 zh title uses the simplified character 宝.
- Chapter 14 has no title in the zh doc (rows 147 and 148 are empty on both sides, the numbering jumps from 十三 at row 144 to 十五 at row 160). This is just an observation.
