## Policies

1. Script and punctuation: Traditional characters in standard (正體) forms, with Buddhist terms of art in CBETA spelling (眾, 說, 迴向, 剎土), full-width punctuation (，。、；：？！), 「」 for quotation and 《》 for titles, Chinese numerals inside terms (三界, 十方, 第四品); no simplified or Japanese forms and no half-width punctuation, because the registers fix Traditional script and the readers use it.
2. A glossary entry holds the bare term: no aspect particles (了, 著), no measure words and no sentence punctuation; a children's phrase may carry 的 only when it is a noun phrase; this keeps entries pasteable into any line of verse.
3. Academic register, order of authority: (a) the terms listed in registers-zh.md; (b) the established Chinese rendering of the same Sanskrit term in the Chinese Buddhist canon (Prajñāpāramitā sūtras and Tibetan-derived translations); (c) current Mandarin usage in Traditional-character Buddhist publications; (d) the Gemini zero-shot line only as evidence of readability or as a tie-breaker, because a zero-shot line drifts from line to line and has no term discipline.
4. Gemini forms seen in the examples that must not be copied: 緣覺 (use 獨覺), 一切智智 for ཐམས་ཅད་མཁྱེན་པ་ཉིད (use 一切智), 佛陀 for རྒྱལ་བ (use 勝者), 勇識 for དཔའ (use 勇士), 魔羅 for བདུད (use 魔), 第三品 宣說一切智 (use 基智), phonetic Chinese for the Sanskrit title (keep IAST), and the mixing of 眾生 and 有情; each is either a different term or inconsistent.
5. Beings: སེམས་ཅན (sentient beings) is 有情 and འགྲོ་བ / འགྲོ (beings) is 眾生, so the English split survives; 有情 is the sattva word of the Prajñāpāramitā corpus and fits the already fixed 菩薩摩訶薩 and 般若波羅蜜多; children's text uses 眾生 for both because the English children's glossary also does not split them and 有情 is opaque to a child.
6. ཆོས has three senses and each gets its own Chinese word: phenomena is 法 (plural or collective 諸法 / 一切法), the Buddha's teaching is 佛法 (bare 法 only inside the fixed collocations 說法, 聞法, 正法, 法王, 法施), and quality of a Buddha or of virtue is 功德 (善法 when preceded by དགེ་ or དཀར་པོའི་); the sense-3 reading 佛法 is refused so that 佛法 stays unambiguously the teaching.
7. Where the English glossary splits a sense that established Chinese does not (界 for realm and element, 導師 for every Tibetan "Guide", 功德 for guṇa and quality-ཆོས), keep the established Chinese word for both and let the compound partner disambiguate; an invented word would be less attested than the shared one.
8. Epithets of the Buddha get one fixed Chinese word per Tibetan epithet and are never collapsed into 佛 or into each other: རྒྱལ་བ 勝者, བདེ་གཤེགས / བདེ་བར་གཤེགས་པ 善逝, དེ་བཞིན་གཤེགས(་པ) 如來, འདྲེན་པ / རྣམ་འདྲེན / རྣམ་པར་འདྲེན་པ / ཡོངས་འདྲེན 導師, མགོན 怙主 (འཇིག་རྟེན་མགོན 世間怙主), སྟོན་པ 大師, བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས 世尊; the epithet is what the Tibetan chose to say and the commentaries read the choice.
9. ཡུམ as an epithet of the perfection of wisdom is 佛母 when bare and 〈owner〉之母 when the Tibetan names the owner (勝者之母, 善逝之母, 如來之母), never 母親 in the academic register; the owner is part of the epithet.
10. Titles of the work: 《》 around a title built from fixed parts, 般若波羅蜜多 for the perfection of wisdom, 攝頌 for "verse summary" (sdud pa tshigs su bcad pa), 聖 for འཕགས་པ, and 寶德藏 for ratnaguṇasaṃcaya as in the Chinese canon's title of this sūtra; the Sanskrit title line is kept in IAST, not transliterated into Chinese characters.
11. Names take the established Chinese name, not a fresh transliteration: 帝釋 (བརྒྱ་བྱིན), 須菩提 (རབ་འབྱོར), 文殊師利 (འཇམ་དཔལ; kids 文殊), 彌勒, 無熱惱池 (མ་དྲོས་མཚོ), 恆河, 南贍部洲 (འཛམ་བུའི་གླིང), 須彌山, 阿羅漢 etc.; the registers ask for common Chinese names and a new transliteration would be unrecognisable.
12. The three knowledges are 一切相智 (རྣམ་པ་ཐམས་ཅད་མཁྱེན་པ་ཉིད), 道智 (ལམ་ཐམས་ཅད་ཤེས་པ་ཉིད) and 基智 (གཞི་ཐམས་ཅད་ཤེས་པ), as fixed in registers-zh.md; the ཐམས་ཅད of the Tibetan is carried by the fixed names, and 一切智 is kept for ཐམས་ཅད་མཁྱེན་པ་ཉིད "omniscience" so that 基智 is never confused with omniscience.
13. The eight topics (八事) that title the sections are rendered as 第N品 for སྐབས་N་པ and 宣說 for བསྟན་པ: 一切相智, 道智, 基智, 一切相圓滿現觀, 頂圓滿現觀, 漸次, 一剎那圓滿現證菩提, 法身; 現觀 renders rtogs pa (abhisamaya), 現證菩提 renders byang chub pa (abhisambodhi), and 圓滿 stands before them as the Tibetan puts rdzogs par there, so the three "complete" titles stay in one pattern.
14. The five aggregates use the established one-character names 色, 受, 想, 行, 識 (གཟུགས, ཚོར(་བ), འདུ་ཤེས, སེམས་པ in the aggregate list, རྣམ་ཤེས / རྣམ་པར་ཤེས(་པ)) and 蘊 for ཕུང་པོ; འདུ་ཤེས outside the aggregate list stays 想 (e.g. 有情想), so the list reads as the standard list.
15. The perfections use the registers' set 布施, 持戒, 安忍, 精進, 靜慮 with 般若 for ཤེས་རབ, each followed by 波羅蜜多 when the Tibetan has ཕ་རོལ་ཕྱིན; ཕ་རོལ་ཏུ་ཕྱིན་པ, ཕ་རོལ་ཕྱིན and ཕ་རོལ་ཕྱིན་པ are one term, and so are all spelling variants, truncated and misspelled forms in the overview (e.g. བྱང་ཆུབ་སེམས་དཔ, བྱང་ཆུང་སེམས་དཔའ, སངས་རྒྱས་ཅོས), because they are the same word.
16. Privative and negated terms are built from the base term: མེད (without) is 無 in the academic register (無相, 無願, 無生, 無我) and 沒有 or 不 in children's phrases, སྟོང་པ་ཉིད / སྟོང་ཉིད is 空性, འདུས་བྱས / འདུས་མ་བྱས is 有為 / 無為, and the three doors of liberation are 空, 無相, 無願 (解脫門); this follows the established three-door formula.
17. Children's register: a technical term becomes a short everyday phrase naming the same thing, never a different idea (空 stays 空, not "nothing"); Sanskrit-derived forms are replaced by native words (波羅蜜多 becomes 圓滿, 菩提 becomes 覺悟, 蘊 becomes 組成部分); transparent or already everyday words stay (菩薩, 如來, 佛法, 導師, 世尊, 空性, 乘, 大乘); and the same child-phrase is used every time so a young reader meets one word per idea.
18. Compounds take their parts' renderings in the Tibetan order of the parts (བྱང་ཆུབ་ཡོན་ཏན 菩提功德, སངས་རྒྱས་ཡེ་ཤེས 佛本智, ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས་ཕ་རོལ་ཕྱིན 持戒波羅蜜多), and an established Chinese compound (法性, 法界, 菩提心, 十力, 四靜慮, 三界, 方便善巧, 魔事) is used only when its parts agree with the table; where they do not, the table wins and the glossary note records the established form.
19. An agent that must depart from this sheet, or that meets a term the sheet does not cover and for which no established Chinese term is known, writes the best candidate and marks the entry ⚑ with the reason; it never changes a core-term rendering silently, because the core terms are the stems of the compounds.
