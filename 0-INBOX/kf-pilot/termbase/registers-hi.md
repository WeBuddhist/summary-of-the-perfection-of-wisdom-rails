# Registers for the Ratnaguṇasaṃcayagāthā Hindi versions (Devanagari)

## Academic (शैक्षणिक संस्करण)
- Readers: Hindi-reading students, practitioners and scholars of Buddhist texts.
- Modern standard Hindi in Devanagari; precise and faithful.
- Terminology: the established Sanskrit (tatsama) Buddhist terms used in Hindi Buddhist scholarship (प्रज्ञापारमिता, बोधिसत्त्व महासत्त्व, सुगत, तथागत, शून्यता, निमित्त, अनुपलम्भ, स्कन्ध, सर्वाकारज्ञता, मार्गज्ञता, वस्तुज्ञता, श्रावक, प्रत्येकबुद्ध, अर्हत्, स्रोतआपन्न, मार, दान, शील, क्षान्ति, वीर्य, ध्यान, क्लेश); proper names in their Sanskrit form (सुभूति, शारिपुत्र, मैत्रेय, मञ्जुश्री, शक्र). Avoid Hindu-devotional or Urdu-Persian substitutes for technical terms.
- One locked rendering per term (per sense).

## Children's (बच्चों का संस्करण)
- Readers: children aged 8–12 reading Hindi.
- Simple everyday Hindi; short common words. A technical idea becomes a simple phrase that keeps the meaning, never a different idea.
- Names kept (बुद्ध, सुभूति, शारिपुत्र); technical Sanskrit compounds replaced by plain phrases.
- One rendering per term (per sense), used every time.
