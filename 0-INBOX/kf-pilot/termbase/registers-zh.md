# Registers for the Ratnaguṇasaṃcayagāthā Chinese versions (Traditional characters)

## Academic (學術版)
- Readers: Chinese-reading students, practitioners and scholars of Buddhist texts.
- Modern written Chinese (白話書面語), Traditional characters, full-width punctuation.
- Terminology: use the established Chinese Buddhist term where one exists (般若波羅蜜多, 菩薩摩訶薩, 善逝, 如來, 空性, 相, 無所緣, 蘊, 一切相智, 道智, 基智, 聲聞, 獨覺, 阿羅漢, 預流, 魔, 布施, 持戒, 安忍, 精進, 靜慮, 煩惱). Where the Tibetan distinguishes something a single Chinese word would blur (e.g. the senses of ཆོས), choose the established term that keeps the distinction (法 / 諸法, 佛法), following the English glossary's sense split.
- One locked rendering per term (per sense).

## Children's (兒童版)
- Readers: children aged 8–12 reading Chinese.
- Plain everyday modern Chinese, Traditional characters. Short words a child knows; a technical idea becomes a simple phrase that keeps the meaning, never a different idea.
- Names: the common Chinese name (佛陀, 須菩提, 舍利子, 彌勒); no transliterated technical Sanskrit terms (not 阿耨多羅三藐三菩提 — say it simply).
- One rendering per term (per sense), used every time.
